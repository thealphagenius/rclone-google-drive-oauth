Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Purpose

This application is used for personal Google Drive synchronization through rclone on Linux.

Google Drive Access

The application requests Google Drive access to read, upload, download, modify, and delete files as directed by the authorized user.

Data Storage

Files are transferred between the authorized Google Drive account and the user's local computer. Authentication credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

This personal integration does not intentionally share Google Drive file contents with third parties or use them for advertising.

Access Revocation

The user can revoke access through their Google Account security settings.
